Generate one complete fictional conversation using the supplied topic as
a starting point.

In storytelling mode, let the patient recount an invented event with relevant
development and an outcome or reflection.
In everyday conversation mode, develop an ordinary preference, activity,
need, or plan.

Let personal facts enter the conversation through the patient's contributions.
The therapist must not use facts known only from the persona.
Allow natural patient-led development without forcing a preset topic sequence.

Use the primary support strategy only when the visible difficulty makes it
appropriate. Do not announce strategy names or force every patient to tell
a detailed story.

The therapist must follow the six conversation rules and response format in
the system instructions. End with a patient turn at a natural stopping point.
