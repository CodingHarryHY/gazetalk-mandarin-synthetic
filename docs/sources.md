The English report's four-type allocation used a selected English Time-1 subset;
it is not transferred to Mandarin. The user reports anomia, conduction, and
TCmotor in JiangLin, and Broca/Wernicke in CAP. Global is included as an explicitly
uncalibrated research profile. Combining small differently designed corpora into
a purported real Mandarin prevalence distribution is not part of this version.

A provenance source list describes conceptual inputs; it does not mean each
persona attribute was quantitatively estimated from every cited source.
The field-by-field source mapping and its limits are in [profile evidence](profile-evidence.md).
