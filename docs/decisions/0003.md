# ADR-0003: Variable-length single-request conversations

## Context
Six fixed turns do not provide the desired space for conversational development and
support. The user requested 10-40 turns and a complete topic-focused conversation.

## Decision
Generate the whole dialogue in a single request. Configure inclusive minimum and
maximum turns; start with therapist and alternate, permitting either final speaker.
Use everyday conversation and personal storytelling. Use only the five approved
support strategies, with natural hesitation and repair rather than fixed event quotas.

## Alternatives considered
A staged v2 generator would change the methodological baseline. Fixed long turns
would encourage padding. Requiring every repair to succeed would create unrealistic
improvement. The length bounds are engineering settings, not therapy dosage.

## Consequences, evidence limits, and reconsideration
Single-shot therapist foreknowledge and formulaic endings remain possible.
Conduction and TCmotor differences may be hard to observe without relevant tasks.
Automated structure checks cannot establish semantic completion. Inspect pilot
samples and length distributions before expansion.
