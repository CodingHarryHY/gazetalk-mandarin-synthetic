## Context
The requested first batch has 50 personas and 40 dialogues each across six profiles.
The original 400-persona construction algorithm is unavailable.

## Decision
Use English authored catalogs and fixed-seed sampling. Retain the English persona
field structure, with explicit field-level provenance. Use mild/moderate/severe
simulation bands and per-profile authored allowed sets. Use 9/9/8/8/8/8 persona
quotas; seed determines the two extra slots. The full job count is 2,000.

## Alternatives considered
LLM-generated biographies would obscure value provenance. Exact six-way balance
would require changing 50 to a multiple of six, or varying dialogues per persona.
Both would depart from the accepted scale. No reference-prevalence allocation is used.

## Consequences, evidence limits, and reconsideration
Age, occupation, severity mix, support preferences, and fatigue are research
settings rather than estimates. Within-profile severity cycling is a coverage
choice. Tests check determinism, unique identities, quotas, and job coverage.
Reconsider if clinical reviewers identify incompatible combinations.
